### General
- Never hard-wrap; no manual newlines mid-sentence.
- Do NOT use em dashes or en dashes as punctuation; such as for clarifications.
- Use wikilinks.

### Avoid slop
- Slop: meaningless, incorrect, one off, irrelevant, unconventional (does not follow document's conventions), out of scope, outdated/stale, repeated facts/constructions/code
- AVOID SLOP AT ALL COST
- When you edit a document, reconcile what you write with the rest: patch or delete stale lines, do not append a new version beside the old, don't leave behind facts that are now obsolete.

### Markdown
- Do not number sections; to cite/refer to sections use wikilinks like [[document#section]] or [[#section]] if in same document.

### Latex
- Do NOT write more than one sentence per line. Splitting a sentence accross lines is fine if there is a good reason to do it (eg keep a long math formula separate).

### Paper references
- When citing a math paper, look for bibtex entries in MR Lookup https://mathscinet.ams.org/mrlookup.
