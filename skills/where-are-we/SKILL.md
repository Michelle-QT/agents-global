---
name: where-are-we
description: "Mid-stage status: summarize what has happened, what is happening, and what is next, as itemized facts. Use when the user asks where we are, what we are up to, or for a status/standup mid-stage."
---

- Answer the "what are we up to" question in the middle of a stage
- Scope to the current stage: work since the last spine commit, plus the live plan.
- Sections (drop any that are empty):
	- **Done** (this stage): completed and verified; flag anything unverified as preliminary.
	- **Now**: the one thing in progress.
	- **Next**: the immediate next steps within this stage.
	- **Open**: blockers or unresolved questions.
- Report facts, not conclusions; if the state is ambiguous, say so rather than guess.
